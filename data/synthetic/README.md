# data/synthetic — SYNTHETIC cases (not real observations or forecasts)

Every file here carries the global attribute `synthetic = "true"`. Everything is generated by
`python -m synth.generate` (`make data-synth`) with fixed seeds. The NetCDF files are not in git
because they are reproducible; `labels.json` files are committed.

| case | hazard | background (real ERA5, extremes soft-clipped) | init (lead 0) |
|---|---|---|---|
| cyc_01..cyc_04 | tropical_cyclone (track = jittered real IBTrACS NI storm) | Amphan window May 2020, real Amphan vortex removed | 2020-05-10 .. 05-14 |
| heat_01..heat_04 | heat_dome (+4..+8 K, 5-12 d) | ARCO-ERA5 May-Jun 2024 | 2024-05-15 .. 06-05 |
| cold_01..cold_04 | cold_wave (-4..-8 K, advected from NW over the plains) | ARCO-ERA5 Dec 2022-Jan 2023 | 2022-12-20 .. 2023-01-08 |
| amphan_replay | tropical_cyclone on the exact IBTrACS Amphan track and intensity | the same real ERA5 dates, with ERA5's own vortex removed | 2020-05-15T06 |

## Files per case
- `truth_5km.nc`: G5 0.04° (999×999), dims (step=41, latitude, longitude), lead 0..240 h every 6 h.
  Variables `t2m, u10, v10, msl, tp` (+ `r850` for cyclones) are int16 packed at 0.01 K, 0.01 m/s,
  1 Pa, 0.02 mm and 0.01 %. `event_mask` (uint8) is the exact event mask.
- `fcst_12km.nc`: G12 0.12° (333×333), dims (number=20, step=41, latitude, longitude).
  - Members are packed at 0.05 K, 0.05 m/s, 5 Pa and 0.05 mm.
  - `<var>_truth` (float32) is the 3×3 block average of the decoded truth_5km, so
    avgpool(5 km) == 12 km exactly (see tests/test_conservation.py).
  - `event_mask_truth` marks cells where at least half of the G5 mask is set.
  - `event_mask` holds each member's own injected-event mask, false alarms included.
  - `member_role` is 0 normal, 1 miss, 2 false_alarm.
- `labels.json`:
  - hazard, init/valid times and the mask definition;
  - the per-lead truth track, with the analytic centre, mask centroid, area, bbox and intensity;
  - the 4-D box (lat, lon, level, time) and the peak value;
  - generator parameters;
  - per-member roles, tracks, miss lead and false-alarm track.

Variable names follow the cfgrib/TIGGE conventions used for NEPS-G GRIB (`t2m, u10, v10, msl, tp,
r`). Each variable also carries a `neps_g_shortName` attribute (`2t, 10u, 10v, msl, tp, r`), so real
NEPS-G can drop in.

## Mask definitions
- cyclone: injected MSLP depression ≤ −4 hPa
- heat dome: injected T2m anomaly ≥ +3 K
- cold wave: injected T2m anomaly ≤ −3 K
